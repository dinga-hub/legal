Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. The Table (life totals, commander damage, counters) works completely offline with no account. An account is only needed for Decks, Player Area, and Groups, so your data syncs across devices and your playgroup can see shared stats.

I can't sign in with Google or Apple.

Make sure you have a working internet connection and that you're not blocking sign-in popups. If it still fails, try signing in with email instead, and email us the exact error message you see.

My commander's bracket looks wrong.

Bracket detection is based on the official Commander Bracket System (1–5) and the Game Changers list from the Rules Committee. If a commander was imported from another site, its listed bracket may lag behind a recent rules update — you can always set it manually when building the deck.

I paid for a subscription but features are still locked.

Open the app's Account settings and tap Restore Purchases. Your subscription is tied to the App Store or Play Store account used to purchase it, so make sure you're signed into the same one. If it still doesn't unlock, email us and we'll sort it out.

Does the app work offline?

The Table works fully offline. Decks, Groups, and Player Area need a connection to sync, but recently viewed data is cached so the app keeps working with a spotty connection.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email support.dingalabs@gmail.com and we'll delete your account and associated data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
